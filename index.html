About Us

Roofing materials you can build on.

Best Roofing Materials Limited supplies aluminum roofing sheets and stone-coated roofing tiles to homeowners, builders and developers across Nigeria, from our base in Mulero, Iyana Ipaly, Lagos.

Beyond materials, our team handles installation, wooden work and iron work — bringing the roof, the frame and the finish under one roof, quite literally.
